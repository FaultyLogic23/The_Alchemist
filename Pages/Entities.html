Timeline

Events

  1. 1994

    Publication of the comic book adaptation

    Comic book adaptation published by Alexandre Jubran.

  2. 1997

    Release of The Alchemist's Symphony

    Musical work by Walter Taieb, released with the support of Paulo Coelho, who wrote an original text for the CD booklet.

  3. 2002

    First theatrical adaptation

    Theatrical adaptation produced and performed in London.

  4. 2006

    Release of the track "The Alchemist"

    Adaptation of the story by the Singapore indie rock band Mistaken Identity.

  5. 2008

    Purchase of the film rights by The Weinstein Company

    The project stalled after the 2017 Harvey Weinstein sexual abuse scandal.

  6. 2009

    Launch of the Indian stage version

    Ninety-minute version commissioned by Ashvin Gidwani from Deepa Gahlot after a London performance.

  7. 2010

    Publication of The Alchemist: A Graphic Novel

    Adapted by Derek Ruiz, with artwork by Daniel Sampere.

  8. 2016

    Acquisition of the film rights by PalmStar Media

  9. 2023

    Acquisition of the film, television and ancillary rights by Legendary Entertainment

    Film adaptation to be made with TriStar Pictures and PalmStar Media, with Jack Thorne attached as a writer.

  10. 2024

    Announcement of the manga adaptation

    Announced by Kadokawa Corporation, illustrated by Tamaki Nakamura, for the Kadokawa Masterpiece Comics label.
